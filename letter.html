A Letter for My Bou 💌

My dearest Mumitu,

Happy Birthday, my Maya Boti. If I could give you one thing today, I would give you the chance to see yourself through my eyes. Then you would understand how precious you are to me, how your smile can soften my hardest days, and how even the smallest moments with you become memories I want to keep forever.

I may not always find the perfect words, and I may not always know the perfect way to show it, but my heart is full of love for you. I want to stand beside you through your happy days, your difficult days, your dreams, and all the little things in between.

On your birthday, I wish you peace in your heart, reasons to smile every day, and the courage to reach every dream you carry. I hope life treats you gently, and I hope I get to keep cheering for you and loving you along the way.

You are not just part of my favourite memories—you are someone I want beside me while we make many more.

With all my love,
Your Arif ❤️
